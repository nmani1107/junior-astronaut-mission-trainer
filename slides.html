What it is

A game that teaches how astronauts train

  • For kids ages 7–12 — play as a young astronaut getting ready for a real mission.
  • Learn how astronauts prepare for Moon and Mars missions: spacewalks, Moon base life, space weather, and flying to the Moon.
  • Every mission teaches a real rule of space life — and every choice has a consequence.
  • Playable in any browser — no download, no account, nothing to install. Your progress stays on your own device.
The missions · 1 of 2

Three missions to train your body and your base

🧑‍🚀 Spacewalk

Catch the falling tools you need — and dodge the falling asteroids and rocks. One wrong catch can cost a heart!

🏃 Exercise in Space

Tap the glowing pad as fast as you can. Astronauts exercise about 2 hours a day, because without gravity bones and muscles get weak.

🌙 Moon Base Keeper

Keep the base alive! Balance oxygen, food, power and your radiation shield — every helper costs power, so every choice is a trade-off.

The missions · 2 of 2

Weather, light, and the flight home

☀️ Space Weather Watch

Watch real NASA motions — a launch, a solar flare, an aurora — and name what you see. Then read live space weather from NASA.

💡 Moon Base Defender

Clear the friendly Moon Dust and bring light back to the base. Answer questions right to get stronger and spread the light.

🚀 Earth to Moon Journey

Blast off, steer your spaceship to the Moon, fix a problem in flight, meet your Moon Friends — then fly home.

Real NASA data

Not made-up space — real NASA data

  • ● LIVE  Today's Astronomy Picture of the Day and a real Mars rover photo, fetched fresh from api.nasa.gov every time you open the game.
  • ● LIVE  Real solar-flare and storm reports from NASA's DONKI space-weather system power Space Weather Watch.
  • RESEARCHED FACTS  RadLab, OSDR, Mars Trek and CSA toolkits used as real researched facts — always labeled, never called live.
  • If the live feed is down, backup real NASA facts appear instead — the game always works.
Built by a family

Designed by kids, built by our whole family

  • Thanesh, 10, and Mounaashree, 8, designed the games and tested every mission — their ideas made the game harder, funnier, and more like real space.
  • All the instruction voices are our family's own recordings — the kids and parents recorded them at home.
  • Dad Manikandan and mom Gracy helped build the game, so the whole family trained together.
  • Every mission starts with the science behind it — so players learn before they play.
